## Sales truth policy

This catalog contains proposed offers and starting prices. They are not evidence of existing customers or revenue.

Never state:
- that a client bought an offer unless there is a recorded transaction;
- that a system is production-ready unless it has actually been tested to that standard;
- that EVEZ has professional experience with a tool unless the evidence supports the claim;
- that an audit is a security certification;
- that an agent is autonomous beyond its demonstrated boundaries.

## Acquisition sequence

1. Public opportunity identified.

3. Relevant repository evidence mapped.
4. Proposal drafted.
5. Human reviews claims, price, availability and commitment.
6. Human submits/contact the buyer.
7. Buyer accepts trial/diagnostic.
8. Scope and payment are confirmed.
9. Work begins.
10. Deliverable and evidence are recorded.
11. Maintenance/expansion is proposed after successful delivery.
