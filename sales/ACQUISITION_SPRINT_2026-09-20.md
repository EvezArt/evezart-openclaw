## Current portfolio truth standard

Every claim must be one of:
OBSERVED, VERIFIED-LIVE, PERSONAL PROJECT, INFERRED, UNKNOWN, or NOT VERIFIED.

A public listing's budget is not income.
A proposal draft is not an application.
A demo plan is not a recording.
A personal project is not a client deployment.
A repository is evidence of code, not automatically evidence of production reliability.
